05 / Our Story

Tradition,
made relevant.

WearVibes is a contemporary fashion brand built around the meeting point of Indian heritage and modern design. The brand celebrates traditional craftsmanship, intricate embroidery and handwoven fabrics while translating them into silhouettes made for the modern woman.

Our point of view

High-quality ethnic wear at accessible prices, with craftsmanship and contemporary styling at the centre.

Our design language

Classic Indian techniques are paired with modern cuts, versatile styling and details that work across occasions.

Our ambition

To grow from a modern ethnic-wear label into a versatile fashion brand spanning occasion and regular wear while staying rooted in Indian culture.

06 / Sustainability

Better choices,
beautifully made.

WearVibes has publicly described sustainability as an important part of its evolution — from eco-friendly fabrics and natural dyes to reducing production waste, recyclable packaging and working with artisans who follow ethical practices.

Materials

A focus on eco-friendly fabrics and more responsible material choices wherever practical.

Process

Efficient production is used to reduce waste while preserving the craftsmanship that gives each piece its character.

Packaging

Minimal and recyclable packaging is part of the brand's stated sustainability direction.

People

WearVibes has highlighted collaboration with artisans who follow ethical practices and help keep traditional craft relevant.
